# DAY FIVE LEARNING
**1. INNER JOIN** - *matching records from both tables only.*

**2. LEFT JOIN** - *all records from the Left Table and only matching records from Right Table and the ones not matching are NULLS.*

**3. RIGHT JOIN** - *all records from the Right Table and only matching records from Left Table and the ones not matching are NULLS.*

**4. FULL OUTER JOIN** - *all records in both tables are made available*
